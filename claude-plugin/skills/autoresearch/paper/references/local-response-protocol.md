# Fixed-template local response identification, schema 1

`local_response_identification` is a bounded protocol for the ER-LA local slice.
It does not turn arbitrary vector experiments into scalar paired cases. Schema 1
fixes 12 actions, 11 simplex tangent coordinates, three independent instances per
phase, two nested states per instance, three repeats and 2,160 request slots.
These are this protocol's declared design limits, not general scientific defaults.
Changing them needs a new validated protocol version.

Use a separate `experiment_designs` registry entry with its own `id`, path, hash,
protocol and claim. The same collection shape used by paired-case designs binds
results, evidence sets and numerical writing assertions. Scientific interpretation
and original obligation acceptance remain separate from software validation.

## Offline preparation and admission

`artifact --name local-response-design-check --packet <packet>` accepts
`{"design":"relative/design.json"}` and reports draft schema/source checks only.
It does not initialize a run, reserve resources, approve a model or certify the
semantic labels. A draft model binding cannot pass formal recording admission.
Formal plan/recording also refuses an explicitly blocked scientific design.

Use `artifact --name experiment-template` for a resolved design. Its envelope
requires caller-supplied dispatch, controller admission and unique request/session
identity. It records development first, freezes coefficients, records prospective
centers, freezes predictions, then records the registered prospective joints.
There is no automatic retry or provider fallback. A lost outcome stays unknown.
Provider transport and usage settlement remain the task adapter's responsibility.

## Evidence and statistics

The recorder preserves every planned slot and an ordered hash chain of starts,
terminal responses and both freezes. Real responses require original byte files,
matching provider/client identities, input-payload hashes, source receipts and
explicit timestamp provenance. Such local consistency checks are not cryptographic
proof that a provider or operator is honest. Unsupported or skewed provider clocks
must not be silently replaced with invented timestamps.

The local result index binds all events, source/public-state dependencies and
provider byte/receipt artifacts. The collection validator re-reads and recomputes
these artifacts; export must include the complete dependency closure. A scalar
result or a different design's events cannot fill a missing local record.

For each probe, means condition on at least two valid responses out of three.
Invalid and failed slots remain in the denominator. Unknown or unstarted slots
prevent complete acquisition. Repeated-input diagnostics also group identical
semantic payloads across registered probe slots. Confidence variation is reported
without inventing an additional threshold. `none` and E confidence remain null.

Central differences retain their signs and true endpoint distances. Other memory
templates share the correct-160 q slopes as an unverified modeling assumption.
Prospective centers change only the intercept. Illegal predicted probability or
confidence vectors fail without clipping. Every joint point keeps its own error
and engineering-tolerance verdict; a pooled mean cannot conceal a failed point.
No global confidence interval, full free-text state identification or stability
certificate follows from these diagnostics.

## Displays and claims

`statistics` emits an evidence audit sheet in PDF or SVG, a prospective-point table,
a complete JSON companion and an input/output trace. The sheet is a diagnostic
artifact, not automatic TMLR publication layout approval. Signed q and confidence
columns, per-point prediction error and repeated-input policy variation are shown;
full vectors, valid/planned counts and cross-slot grouping stay in the companion.
The 0.10 lines are engineering tolerances, not confidence intervals.

Numerical assertions name the operating point and the measured probe or derivative
axis/output coordinate. Supported measures are `same_input_tv_max`,
`prospective_policy_tv`, `prospective_confidence_abs_error` and
`central_difference_entry`. Units and nested valid/planned denominators must match.
Derivative assertions bind both registered endpoints in minus/plus order.
`confidence` is a reserved output coordinate, not a legal action ID.

Software fixtures are explicitly labeled and cannot supply actual research findings
or independent manuscript reviews. A successful export checks artifact consistency;
it does not satisfy unresolved scientific or publication obligations.
