knowledge. Exact source identities and hashes are in provenance.json. It does
not include Deli's unlicensed third-party HTML or full original protocol, infer
an MIT grant over that text, or ship another project's runtime/state engine.
The three copied user-local helpers and tests retain their own source manifest
and attribution under vendor/deli-local/NOTICE.md. Other checks and knowledge
files synthesize methods from the sources listed in provenance.json.
