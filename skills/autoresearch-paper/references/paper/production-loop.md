# Autonomous paper production loop

Run all stages autonomously inside the frozen Validated Research Package and
Claim Boundary. Maintain a traceability ledger throughout.

## Literature

Verify the bibliographic identity and relevance of each source. Use literature
to explain context, distinguish the frozen contribution, and support statements
about prior work. Prefer primary sources and record verification status. Do not
reopen broad novelty discovery, satisfy a paper-count quota, or infer a stronger
claim from later work.

## Structure

Translate venue requirements and the Claim Boundary into an outline. Assign
each scientific statement to supporting evidence and each limitation to the
relevant scope. Make negative, qualified, and insufficient evidence visible.
Order the argument for comprehension, not to conceal contrary results.

## Grounded Writing

Draft from the traceability ledger. Keep method descriptions consistent with
frozen code and configuration. Preserve exact evaluation conditions, baseline
scope, uncertainty, and claim status. Separate observation from interpretation
and interpretation from speculation. Label limitations directly.

Never turn a desired explanation into evidence. A reviewer-suggested analysis
that could change a claim is new research, even if it could be expressed as one
paragraph.

## Figures and tables

Reuse validated artifacts where suitable. Otherwise rerun only frozen
deterministic tasks, or compute disclosed statistics and visual encodings from
existing data. Record source files, filters, transformations, commands, and
outputs so each value is reproducible.

Do not add seeds, ablations, experiments, evaluator variants, data subsets, or
comparisons that could change the Claim Boundary. Treat a request for such an
artifact as out of scope, not as missing polish.

## Compilation

Build the venue format. Resolve citations, cross-references, floats, fonts,
anonymity, page limits, supplements, and accessibility text. Inspect the
rendered artifact rather than trusting source alone. Remove unresolved markers,
clipped content, illegible graphics, and accidental identity leaks.

## Internal routing

Send citation findings to Literature, argument gaps to Structure, unsupported
or inconsistent prose to grounded Writing, numerical or visual defects to
Figures/Tables, and rendering defects to Compilation. Repeat the responsible
stage and preserve the review trail until every release gate is clean.
